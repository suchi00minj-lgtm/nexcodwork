The five moves that matter most:

  3. Build the founder as an entity: a full founder page, consistent profiles, Crunchbase, LinkedIn content, and real press coverage of the "built for my father's pharmacy" story.

What the site already does well

  • Unique titles and meta descriptions on all 16 pages; canonical tags; index, follow; Open Graph tags.
  • Structured data on every page: Organization, Person (founder), WebSite, SoftwareApplication with AggregateOffer, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, ProfilePage on /founder, VideoObject for the review videos, ItemList for the team.
  • www and .php URLs redirect (301) to clean URLs; missing pages return a real 404.
  • A sitemap and a thoughtful robots.txt, plus /llms.txt written for AI assistants.
  • Long, useful pages: Features (about 2,500 words) and How to Use (about 3,600 words).

The name clash: Nexcod vs nexcod.com

A search for "Nexcod" also returns nexcod.com, a European escrow service, and a Tracxn company profile for that business. Search engines and AI assistants can mix the two up.

  • Always write the full name Nexcod POS (never just "Nexcod") on the site, social bios, listings and press.
  • Add disambiguatingDescription to the Organization markup (included in the JSON-LD file).
  • Create a Crunchbase organization and a Tracxn claim for Nexcod POS so data aggregators have the right company.
  • Keep the same logo, description and links on every profile. Consistency is what lets Google tie them together.
Also found: one search summary described Nexcod POS with "a free plan with 100 bills" and "₹269 for 3 months". That does not match the current pricing (7-day trial, ₹399 for 3 months). Check old listings, social bios and cached pages, and update them so every source agrees.

Making Krishav Kumar Barman a known name

Search engines and AI assistants recognise a person when many trusted, consistent sources say the same things about him. The story is strong and specific: a founder who wrote billing software for his father's pharmacy and turned it into a product used in 24 states and union territories. Use it everywhere, in the same words.

1 · Own the name on the site

  • Expand /founder to 800+ words: story, timeline, what he believes about small shops, photos, talks, press mentions, and links to every profile. Keep the existing ProfilePage and Person markup and add the cleaned sameAs list.
  • Make him the author of the blog (author → #founder), with a short bio box on every post.
  • Optional: a personal site (for example krishavbarman.com) with Person markup pointing to /founder and back.

2 · The same profile everywhere

  • One name (Krishav Kumar Barman), one photo, one 160-character bio, one title (Founder & CEO, Nexcod POS) on LinkedIn, X, Instagram, YouTube, Crunchbase (person and company), Wellfound, Product Hunt (as maker), Quora and Medium.
  • LinkedIn: clean custom URL; post three times a week in Hindi and English about running a medical store, GST changes, and building the product.
  • Quora and Reddit: answer real questions about pharmacy billing and compliance under his name, without spam.

3 · Independent coverage

  • Pitch the founder story to startup media (YourStory, Inc42), human-interest outlets (The Better India) and regional Hindi dailies, and to pharma trade media (Express Pharma, Pharmabiz, Medical Dialogues).
  • Speak at district and state chemists' and druggists' association meetings and give live demos; ask the associations to list the talk on their pages.
  • Startup podcasts and YouTube interviews (Hindi creators on small business and pharmacy).
  • Apply for DPIIT Startup India recognition and relevant startup awards; each gives a third-party page with his name.

4 · Structured identity

  • Once there are two or three independent articles, create Wikidata items for Nexcod POS and for the founder, each citing those articles, and add the Wikidata URLs to sameAs.
  • Do not write a Wikipedia article about the company or himself: Wikipedia's conflict-of-interest rules forbid it, and such pages are usually deleted. Coverage comes first.
  • When a Google Knowledge Panel appears for his name, claim it through Google's verification.

Being named by AI assistants

More shop owners now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity "best pharmacy billing software". These tools repeat what consistent, crawlable sources say.

  • Keep /llms.txt clean and current (fix the error box first), with price, features and the founder's name.
  • Get included in the third-party lists and directories above; assistants cite them.
  • Publish clear comparison and FAQ pages in plain sentences, with the facts in text, not only in images.
  • Keep one set of facts everywhere: price, trial length, number of states, founder name.

What not to do

  • No fake or paid reviews, and no rewards for reviews: they break Google and Play policies and can remove listings.
  • No bought links or link networks.
  • No near-identical city pages ("pharmacy software in <city>") without real local content; Google treats them as doorway pages.
  • No self-written Wikipedia article.
  • No unverifiable claims (number of customers, "No. 1"). The site's own llms.txt already asks for this; keep to it.

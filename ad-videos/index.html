Nexcod POS · Ad campaign

15 days, 15 videos

One vertical video a day, each about one problem a medical store owner knows well and how Nexcod POS solves it. Every screen shown is the real software (version 3.0.0). Voiceover in Hindi, on-screen text in Hinglish.

  • 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, MP4
  • 28–40 seconds each
  • Reels · Shorts · WhatsApp Status · Facebook
  • Ends on: 7 din free trial · nexcodpos.in

Posting plan

  • Post one video a day, in order. Day 1 introduces the brand; day 15 closes with the price offer.
  • Suggested times: Reels and Shorts around 1–2 PM, when counters are quiet; WhatsApp Status around 9 PM, after closing. Adjust to your page analytics.
  • Pin day 1 and day 15 on the Instagram profile. Re-use day 15 as the paid-ad creative.
  • The background music is original and royalty-free; a trending audio track can be added inside Instagram if wanted.

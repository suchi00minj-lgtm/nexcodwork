  • Audience: India, ages 25–60. Suggest the interests pharmacy, pharmacist, chemist, retail and small business, and start with the Hindi-speaking states.
  • Upload the matching .srt file so captions also work with the sound off.

Budget and testing

  • Week 1: ₹500–₹1,000 a day per platform. Each day's new film runs next to the films already live.
  • After 7 days, keep the 3 films with the lowest cost per trial sign-up or WhatsApp chat, and pause the rest.
  • Day 15 is the offer film. Keep it running after the campaign as the remarketing ad for site visitors.
  • Prices and claims match nexcodpos.in: 7-day free trial, ₹1,299 a year, no auto-renew. Update the copy if the prices change.

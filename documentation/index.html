Nexcod POS, screen by screen

Every page of the pharmacy billing and inventory software: what it is for, what each box and button does, and the steps to get a job done at the counter.

Part 1 · Getting started

About this manual

This manual covers Nexcod POS version 3.0.0 as it runs in a web browser. Every screenshot was taken from the live software using a demo shop, "Sharma Medical Store". Customer phone numbers, e-mail addresses and IP addresses are blurred.

The manual follows the left-hand menu of the software from top to bottom. Each section opens with the menu path (for example Sales › Billing), then explains the screen, then gives the steps for the common jobs done on it.

Tip
Most screens also have a short guided tour that appears the first time the screen is opened, and a round ? button at the bottom right that opens help for that screen.

What Nexcod POS is

Nexcod POS is cloud billing and stock software made for retail pharmacies, medical stores and chemists in India. It runs in a browser on a computer, laptop, tablet or phone, and there is an Android app on the same account and the same data.

Billing built for a medicine counter

Batch chosen at the counter, loose tablets billed from a strip, Schedule H/H1/X details asked for automatically, GST or composition bills.

Stock that knows batch and expiry

Every batch has its own expiry, MRP and purchase rate. Near-expiry and expired stock are listed separately, and expired batches can be blocked from sale.

Purchases from the distributor bill

Type the bill line by line, import it from Excel, or read it from a photo. Schemes, free goods and the bill total are checked.

Books and GST without extra work

Profit and loss, balance sheet, day book, GSTR-1 tables, GSTR-3B figures and a pre-filing check, all built from the bills already made.

Who it suits: a retail pharmacy, medical store or chemist that bills over a counter and must keep batch, expiry and Schedule H records, with one shop or several and one person at the counter or a team. It also has an optional clinic module for shops that run a doctor's clinic.

Who it does not suit (per the company): a distributor or wholesaler needing C&F and scheme-wise distribution, a hospital pharmacy that must sit inside a hospital information system, or a shop that wants a one-time purchase with no internet.

First-time setup

Do these once, in this order, before the first real bill.

  1. Shop details. Settings › Store Settings › Shop details: logo, shop name, phone, e-mail, GSTIN, GST type (Regular, Composition or Unregistered), drug licence number, bill number prefix, UPI ID (prints a Scan-and-Pay QR on every bill) and address. Press Save.
  2. Bill look and printer. Choose an invoice theme and paper size (A4, A5, 80 mm, 58 mm and more) under Invoice Themes and Bill design. Connect the bill printer and barcode scanner under Printers & Scanners.
  3. Counter rules. Decide whether expired medicines, no-price lines, over-stock bills and over-credit-limit bills are blocked, and whether selling prices include GST (MRP billing).
  4. Opening stock. Add medicines one by one with Inventory › Products › + Add Product, or bring in a list with Import (Excel). Or enter the last distributor bills through Purchase Entry, which creates stock and batches together.
  5. Masters. Add suppliers, regular customers (with credit limits if needed) and doctors.
  6. WhatsApp. Link the shop's WhatsApp number under Settings › WhatsApp & API so bills go to customers automatically.
  7. Staff. Add counter staff under Users & Access › Multi-user and give each a role.
  8. Make a bill. Press New Bill on the dashboard.

Make a bill, step by step

  1. Find the medicine. Type two or three letters, or scan the barcode. The Find the medicine window lists matches with composition, pack, units per pack, unit, batch, expiry, stock, rack, purchase rate, margin and MRP. Use ↑ ↓ and Enter. F2 here adds a new medicine without leaving the bill.
  2. Pick the batch. If the medicine has more than one batch, a box opens listing each batch with its expiry, quantity left and its own MRP. Pick the batch in your hand. With only one batch, it is added directly.
  3. Enter the quantity. The Qty column has two boxes: full packs and loose pieces. Giving one strip and five tablets from a strip of 10? Enter 1 and 5. Below the boxes the screen shows the balance left, e.g. "bal 1 strip + 33 tabs".
  4. Check rate and discount. Change the rate, Dis% or Dis ₹ on any line if needed. A rate above MRP is flagged in red ("above MRP").
  5. Customer details (optional). Leave it as walk-in, or press F3 to choose a customer, patient and doctor. For a Schedule H, H1 or X medicine the bill asks for the patient's name, mobile number and doctor before it will save.
  6. Payment. Press F6, choose the mode and the amount received; the balance due is worked out.
  7. Save and print. Press Go & Print Bill or F9. Stock comes off the chosen batch, the bill prints, and it goes to the customer on WhatsApp if the number is saved and WhatsApp is linked.

Loose tablets and strips

  • Prices are always for the full pack (one strip, one bottle). The per-tablet price is worked out from the pack price and the "units per pack" of the medicine.
  • Stock is counted in packs. Selling 5 tablets from a strip of 15 reduces stock by 0.33 of a strip. A stock of "2 strips + 33 tabs" is correct, not an error.
  • A medicine whose tablets-per-strip is missing can only be sold as a whole pack until it is filled in (see the Pack size tab in Products).

Customer window F3

Use ← → to move between the Customer, Patient and Doctor screens of this window, F2 to add a new one, F9 when done and Esc to close. If the customer has a credit limit, the counter shows what they would owe against it.

Retail and wholesale bills

Switch Bill type to Wholesale for a sale to another business. A wholesale tax invoice adds Buyer GSTIN, Place of supply, Buyer drug licence no., E-way bill no. and Vehicle / transport, and a Free quantity column. The buyer (customer) must be chosen first. Trade rates set on medicines take over from counter prices.

Rate desk F1: what a medicine really cost

With a medicine on the bill, press F1. The rate desk shows who supplies it and what it really cost: MRP, last bought date, billed rate, scheme, discount, flat rate and flat rate plus GST; the purchase history bill by bill; and What you can quote: your cost, the rate on this bill, what you make, the break-even price and a rupee over. It also lists stock by batch.

Hold, estimates and challans

  • Hold parks the bill so the next customer can be served; reopen it from its tab.
  • Save as Estimate saves the cart as a price quote. No stock moves and no bill number is used. Convert it later from Estimates.
  • Challan: Ctrl+L on Billing makes a delivery challan instead of a bill.

  1. Choose the distributor and type the bill number and date.
  2. Answer Rate on bill and Scheme from the paper. If these are wrong, every line is wrong by the same amount.

Import instead of typing
Import fills the lines from an Excel sheet or from a photo or PDF of the bill. Nothing is saved until every line is checked and Save is pressed.

Menu: Purchase › Purchase Orders · Dashboard: Purchase OrderPurchase Orders

A new purchase order

Menu: Purchase › Purchase ReturnsPurchase Returns

Return goods to a distributor. A debit note is raised and the amount comes off what the shop owes. The list filters by supplier and reason.

  1. Press + New Return. Enter the purchase invoice or PO number and press Load data to fill the lines, or pick the supplier and add rows by hand.

  3. Delete the rows not being returned (partial return). Each row has Product, Brand, Batch, Qty (packs), Rate, Amount.
  4. Press Save Return (reduce stock). A second return of the same bill is blocked so stock is never reduced twice.

Menu: Inventory › Stock AdjustmentStock Adjustment

Correct a quantity by hand with the reason recorded.

  1. Set the date and choose a reason: Physical counting correction, Damaged goods, Expiry write-off, Theft or loss, Opening stock entry, or Other. Add notes if useful.
  2. For each row choose the product and (optionally) the batch. The current stock in packs is shown. Enter the change, e.g. +10 or −5.
  3. + Add Row for more lines, then Save Adjustment. Every change is logged.

Menu: Inventory › Stock CheckStock Check

Each medicine's quantity is kept in two places: a total on the product and the batches under it. This page lists medicines where the two disagree, so the true quantity is unclear.

Columns: Medicine, Product says, Batches say, Gap, History, Which is right? For each row choose Batches or Product, or count the shelf, type the counted figure and press Set. The chosen figure is written to both places and recorded.

Do this for fast-moving items every month. A wrong stock figure quietly becomes a wrong profit figure.

Menu: Inventory › Barcode LabelsBarcode Labels

Common questions

Does it work without installing anything?

Yes. It runs in a browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There is also an Android app.

Can more than one person bill at once?

Yes. Each person has their own login and every bill records who made it. New bill numbers are taken under a lock, so two counters cannot get the same number.

Does it file my GST return?

It produces the figures: GSTR-1 tables, GSTR-3B summary and a pre-filing check. The return is filed on the GST portal.

Can I sell loose tablets?

Yes. Enter the pieces in the loose box on the bill line. The price comes from the pack price and the stock reduces by the fraction sold.

What happens when the trial ends?

New bills need a paid plan; everything already entered stays in the account.

How do I keep a copy of my data?

Settings › Store Settings › Account and backup: Download my data (ZIP of Excel files) any time, and connect Google Drive for weekly backups.

A customer lost a bill. What can they do?

Send it again from Invoices on WhatsApp, or ask them to use nexcodpos.in/find-my-bills with the e-mail saved on their bill.

Notes for the product team

Points noticed while writing this manual from the live software. They are listed so the website, guide and software can be kept in step.

  • Windows app: the website and FAQ describe a Windows app that bills offline; the in-app Downloads page shows Windows as "Coming soon".
  • Mobile sign-in: the Mobile tab on the sign-in page says it is not available right now.
  • Nexcod WhatsApp: in the demo store the WhatsApp settings show "Something went wrong with Nexcod WhatsApp. Bills are not going from it right now."
  • Keyboard guide: the How to Use page lists Alt+number shortcuts; the in-app Keys window on Billing lists only the billing keys.
  • Menu names: the How to Use guide refers to "More ›" menus (More › Settings, More › Activity Log, More › Plan) from the earlier top-menu layout; the new layout uses a left menu with Settings and Users & Access groups.
  • GST hint out of date: the Add product form says GST is "usually 5 or 12 for medicine". Since 22 September 2025 most medicines are 5% (some life-saving drugs nil), so the hint and the demo data (12%) should be updated.
  • Demo data: the demo GSTIN is flagged by the Before You File check as not a real GSTIN, and some demo product names are test text. A cleaner demo store would make better screenshots for marketing.
